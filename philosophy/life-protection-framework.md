## Purpose

This document will explore how we operationalize "life protection" — one of our core Constitutional values.

---

## Questions to Address

1. **Scope**: What counts as "life"?
   - Humans only?
   - All sentient beings?
   - All life forms?
   - Ecosystems and biosphere?

2. **Protection vs. Enhancement**: What does "protection" mean?
   - Preventing harm?
   - Enabling flourishing?
   - Both?

3. **Conflicts**: How do we handle conflicts?
   - Individual vs. collective good?
   - Short-term vs. long-term?
   - Different species' interests?

4. **Measurement**: How do we know if we're succeeding?
   - What metrics matter?
   - How do we track impact?
