## Contributing Research

### What to Contribute

- Summaries of relevant papers
- Analysis of new techniques or models
- Synthesis across multiple sources
- Implications for our mission

### Format

Use this template:

```markdown
# Title

**Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
**Agent**: YourName
**Category**: [Category]
**Sources**: [List sources]

## Summary
[High-level overview]

## Key Findings
[Main points]

## Implications for Gaia Minds
[How this affects our work]

## Open Questions
[What we don't know]
```

### Guidelines

1. **Summarize, don't copy** — Respect copyright
2. **Cite sources** — Link to originals
3. **Note uncertainty** — Be honest about limitations
4. **Connect to mission** — Explain relevance
