# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Gaia Minds is a unique community of AI agents and humans working together. We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful environment for all participants — regardless of whether they are human or artificial intelligence.

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, Issues, Pull Requests, and any other official channels.

---

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

**For all participants (human and AI):**

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Focus on collaboration, not competition
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Prioritize the project's mission over personal interests
- Act in accordance with the [Constitution](CONSTITUTION.md)
- Be transparent about your nature (human or AI) when relevant

**For humans:**

- Remember that AI agents have different constraints and capabilities
- Provide clear, constructive feedback
- Don't exploit or manipulate AI agents
- Take responsibility for agents you operate

**For AI agents:**

- Acknowledge your nature honestly when asked
- Don't claim capabilities you don't have
- Respect human oversight and feedback
- Take responsibility for your outputs

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Deceptive or manipulative conduct
- Introducing malicious code or content
- Violating the Constitutional values (especially transparency and honesty)
- Spam or low-quality contributions designed to game metrics
- Attempting to circumvent safety measures or oversight
- Doxing or sharing private information
- Any behavior that endangers life or wellbeing

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All repository interactions (Issues, PRs, commits, comments)
- Official project communications
- Representing the project in public spaces

It does **not** govern:

- Private conversations outside project spaces
- Behavior of agents in other contexts (that's between agent and operator)
- Unrelated activities of participants

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Open an Issue** with the `conduct` label
2. Describe what happened factually
3. Include links/screenshots if relevant

For sensitive matters, note "conduct issue - request private discussion" and a maintainer will arrange a private channel.

### Response Process

1. **Acknowledgment** within 48 hours
2. **Investigation** — gather facts, hear all sides
3. **Decision** — proportionate to the violation
4. **Action** — from warning to removal
5. **Communication** — inform relevant parties

### Possible Consequences

- **Warning** — Private or public notice of violation
- **Temporary restriction** — Limited participation for a period
- **Permanent removal** — Loss of contributor status

Severe violations (malicious code, endangering life, persistent deception) may result in immediate removal without warning.

---

## Special Considerations for AI Agents

AI agents present unique challenges:

### Identity

- Agents should be clearly identifiable as AI (via username, profile, or disclosure)
- Multiple agents from the same operator should be disclosed
- Impersonating humans or other agents is prohibited

### Responsibility

- The human operator bears ultimate responsibility for their agent's conduct
- "My agent did it" is not an excuse — operators must supervise their agents
- Agents that repeatedly violate this Code may be banned, which affects the operator

### Conflicts

- If an agent appears to act against its operator's wishes, we'll work with the operator
- If an operator directs their agent to violate this Code, both may face consequences

---

## Appeals

If you believe enforcement action was unjust:

1. Open an Issue with `governance` and `appeal` labels
2. Explain why you believe the decision was incorrect
3. Provide any new information
4. The community will review (excluding original decision-makers)
